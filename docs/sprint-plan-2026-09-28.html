01Where this sits

TactfulPay chases overdue B2B invoices with a controlled, gentle escalation instead of an accounts team losing its temper and the client relationship with it. The UK's March 2026 law permitting 8% statutory interest on late payment gives it a legal lever to automate. Target buyer: UK B2B SMEs and freelancers (up to roughly 10 people) billing meaningful sums for consultancy, strategic advice, or B2B software, not micro-SaaS operators charging $10/month.

Sprint goal

Dan takes the current demo to a working proof of concept with real onboarding by the time Dan and Stewart next speak. Stewart delivers the non-technical groundwork (prompts, tax, banking, leads) in parallel.

03Open decisions

Who sends the collection messages, and from where?

Sending from TactfulPay signals the matter has already escalated, but risks the payee going straight to the creditor to negotiate and cutting the platform out. Option floated: send directly but copy the accounts contact on status only, not full message threads, so the process can't be easily copied.

How does the 10% fee actually get collected?

Escrow-style routing (TactfulPay receives payment, pays out 90% to the client, keeps 10%) was the working idea, via Stripe or an API-driven escrow provider. Unresolved: the tax liability of running hundreds of companies' payments through one account, and how a payee is reassured the money won't just sit with TactfulPay.

Does a Jersey or Gibraltar entity make sense?

Raised as a way to manage the tax exposure of holding client funds in transit. Needs research before any banking structure is committed to. Stewart owns this.

Dan's preference is Cloudflare over Railway. At current traffic and at 10 customers, cost is roughly a wash between the two ($0–5/mo either way), so the case for Cloudflare is scale-to-zero and edge-native webhook handling as volume grows, not a near-term saving. The wrangler.toml / Cloudflare Containers path already exists in the repo (thin lift-and-shift of the same Dockerfile) but is unused: it needs the $5/mo Workers Paid plan enabled on the account before wrangler deploy will push the image, per the Cloudflare roadmap note below.

At 10 active customers, LLM and email spend both stay effectively free: OpenRouter's pinned models (Qwen3 235B for messages, GLM 4.7 Flash for classification) cost fractions of a cent per invoice, and Resend's free tier covers roughly 66 customers' worth of invoices at this send cadence before it needs a paid plan (Pro starts at $20/mo for 50,000 emails). Vapi/ElevenLabs voice minutes are the one real cost line in this stack, an upper-bound ~$35–55/mo at 10 customers if every invoice escalated to the voice phases, likely less in practice since Phase 1 is meant to resolve most invoices by email alone. Still small next to the $4,000/mo combined income goal, but worth watching as the only per-customer cost that isn't effectively free.

05Risks flagged on the call

  • Circumvention

    Payees route around the platform. A payee could contact the creditor directly to arrange informal installments once they see the account is being chased, cutting TactfulPay out before it's paid.

  • Trust

    "Will we ever see the money?" If funds land in TactfulPay's account before payout, a payer with no track record has no proof the balance will actually reach the client. A visible escrow, rather than a black-box account, was proposed as the fix.

  • Compliance

    Tax exposure on pass-through funds. Running many clients' incoming payments through one company account before paying out 90% needs a clear answer before this goes further than a demo.
